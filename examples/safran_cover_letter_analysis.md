# Safran Cover Letter Analysis

## Why this cover letter worked

1. **Strong contextual opening**
   - The introduction quickly connects candidate value to the target role.

2. **Achievement-backed narrative**
   - Body paragraphs use concrete achievements rather than generic motivation statements.

3. **Balanced tone**
   - Professional and human voice, technically credible without sounding robotic.

4. **Company-targeted close**
   - Final paragraph reinforces fit with Safran's context and the applicant's projected contribution.

## Reuse Rules

- Keep to 3-4 paragraphs.
- Anchor at least two role-relevant achievements.
- Link technical strengths to business or operational outcomes.
- End with a specific motivation statement tied to the target.
