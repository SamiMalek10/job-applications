# Safran Cover Letter Tailoring Notes

Reference letter example: `lettre_Safran_AE_AIEngineer_Sami_Malek.pdf` (repository root).

## Opening hook strategy
- The strongest approach is to open with role-company alignment plus immediate evidence of applied AI impact, rather than generic motivation.

## Achievement led with
- High-impact quantified outcomes are the best lead signals (e.g., 87% retention improvement, 55M MAD opportunity, 98% accuracy), selected based on the job's dominant challenge.

## Company-specific reference
- Effective tailoring references Safran's industrial/aerospace constraints: reliability, production-readiness, and responsible AI behavior.

## Tone calibration
- Formal and professional, with controlled enthusiasm.
- Confident but evidence-based (no over-claiming).
- Language level should remain executive-readable and concrete.

## Why this works as few-shot
- Shows clear mapping from job need → relevant proof.
- Demonstrates concise 3-4 paragraph structure with strong flow.
- Balances technical depth and business value.
- Keeps personalization specific to company context, not generic AI wording.
