## Tone guidelines
- Tone: confident, impact-led, strategic.
- Lead with measurable business/technical outcomes.

## Cover letter scaffold
1. **Opening**: strongest quantified achievement first.
2. **Body 1**: connect relevant experience directly to company challenge.
3. **Body 2**: execution approach and expected value in role context.
4. **Closing**: clear next-step invitation.

## CV priority order (full-time)
1. Impact summary
2. Experience first
3. Highly relevant projects
4. Targeted technical skills
5. Education

## EN conventions
- Crisp and direct.
- Prefer plain business language over generic enthusiasm.
- End with a proactive call to discuss fit.
