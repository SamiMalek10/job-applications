# Template — Job Application (EN)

## Expected Format
- Professional cover letter for a full-time role.
- Confident, concise, value-focused tone.

## Structure

### [HOOK]
- Lead with a relevant achievement, domain insight, or strong match to the company's challenge.
- Avoid generic application formulas.

### [STRONGEST_ALIGNMENT]
- Present the most compelling evidence for the role.
- Tie past outcomes directly to the employer's needs.

### [DIFFERENTIATOR]
- Add a second strength: systems thinking, product mindset, safety/governance awareness, communication, or leadership potential.
- Address any meaningful gap honestly and strategically.

### [CLOSING]
- Show genuine interest in the company.
- Invite the next step.
- End with a polished professional sign-off.

## Reminders
- Keep it tight and specific.
- Prioritize credibility over hype.
