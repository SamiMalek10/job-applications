## Tone guidelines
- Tone: academically credible, intellectually curious, structured.
- Emphasize learning intent and research-minded execution.

## Cover letter scaffold
1. **Opening**: motivation for the program and institutional fit.
2. **Body 1**: evidence from relevant projects/coursework/research.
3. **Body 2**: learning objectives and practical application plan.
4. **Closing**: availability and interest in further discussion.

## CV priority order (training)
1. Education first
2. Certifications
3. Relevant projects
4. Relevant experience
5. Technical skills

## EN conventions
- Clear and formal tone.
- Avoid exaggeration; prioritize evidence and intent.
- Close with a concise interview/discussion request.
