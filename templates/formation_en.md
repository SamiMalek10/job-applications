# Template — Academic Program (EN)

## Expected Format
- Academic motivation letter or statement-style cover letter.
- Sender block, date, recipient/program block, subject line if needed.
- Professional, thoughtful, and academically grounded tone.

## Structure

### [HOOK]
- Open with a strong achievement, insight, or program-specific observation.
- Avoid starting with "I" if a sharper opening is possible.
- Establish fit with the program's intellectual direction.

### [STRONGEST_ALIGNMENT]
- Connect the candidate's most relevant projects/experience to the program's learning and research themes.
- Emphasize technical depth, evidence, and readiness for advanced study.

### [DIFFERENTIATOR]
- Highlight what is rare in the profile: production exposure, systems thinking, engineering rigor, multidisciplinary perspective, or safety/governance mindset.
- Show how the candidate can bridge theory and deployment.

### [CLOSING]
- State why the program matters for the next academic/professional step.
- Mention research or advanced engineering ambitions.
- Close warmly and professionally.

## Reminders
- Keep it concise and evidence-based.
- Do not restate the CV line by line.
- Tailor the program references, not just the domain.
