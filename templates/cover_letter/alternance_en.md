# Cover Letter Template — Apprenticeship — English

## Structure
1. Opening on learning speed plus delivery mindset
2. Paragraph on the strongest practical experience
3. Paragraph on the school/company complementarity
4. Conversational close

## Recommended angle
- Show why academic depth and hands-on execution reinforce each other.

## Personalization slots
- Work-study rhythm if stated
- Team, product, or data environment
- Early contribution promise grounded in evidence
