# Cover Letter Template — Internship — English

## Structure
1. Value-led opening tied to the internship mission
2. Main alignment paragraph
3. Complementary paragraph on projects, stack, or adaptability
4. Short proactive close

## Recommended angle
- Use one strong internship/project outcome to make a junior profile feel low-risk.

## Personalization slots
- Exact internship scope
- Technologies or industry
- Team or mentor if known
