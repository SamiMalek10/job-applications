# Cover Letter Template — Training Program — English

## Structure
1. Opening that links a concrete achievement to academic ambition
2. Paragraph on current background and validated strengths
3. Paragraph on what the program adds beyond existing experience
4. Closing on long-term direction and program fit

## Recommended angle
- Frame the application as a logical next step, not an unfocused pivot.

## Personalization slots
- Modules, research themes, or pedagogy
- Program name, institution, location
- Medium-term career direction
