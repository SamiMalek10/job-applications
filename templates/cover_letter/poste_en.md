# Cover Letter Template — Full-Time Role — English

## Structure
1. Open with a strong result
2. Main alignment paragraph tied to the role's biggest need
3. Differentiator paragraph (systems, governance, BI, etc.)
4. Company-specific close

## Recommended angle
- Write like a future contributor, not like a student asking for an opportunity.

## Personalization slots
- Team pain point
- Product or business challenge
- Specific company signal
