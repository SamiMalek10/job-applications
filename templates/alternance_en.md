# Template — Work-Study / Apprenticeship (EN)

## Expected Format
- Cover letter for a longer-term apprenticeship or work-study role.
- Balance long-term growth potential with near-term contribution.

## Structure

### [HOOK]
- Start from the team's need or the business/technical challenge.
- Show immediate understanding of the role.

### [STRONGEST_ALIGNMENT]
- Use the most relevant hands-on experience or projects.
- Demonstrate how prior execution translates into work-study readiness.

### [DIFFERENTIATOR]
- Highlight discipline, autonomy, communication, and ability to connect coursework with delivery.
- Make the long-term value proposition visible.

### [CLOSING]
- Emphasize interest in growing within the organization.
- Connect the opportunity to the broader career path.
- Close professionally.

## Reminders
- Keep it specific and credible.
- Treat the apprenticeship as a partnership, not only a training request.
