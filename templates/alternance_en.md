## Tone guidelines
- Tone: professional, execution-ready, reliable.
- Balance academic rigor with business delivery.

## Cover letter scaffold
1. **Opening**: role/company fit and motivation.
2. **Body 1**: autonomous project/experience evidence with impact.
3. **Body 2**: apprenticeship rhythm (e.g., 3 weeks company / 1 week school) and immediate contribution plan.
4. **Closing**: availability and interview invitation.

## CV priority order (apprenticeship)
1. Targeted summary
2. Experience and projects (balanced)
3. Education
4. Technical skills

## EN conventions
- Direct and professional.
- Clear mention of work-study rhythm if provided.
- End with action-oriented closing.
