# Resume Template — Apprenticeship — English

## Positioning
- Show the ability to keep learning while delivering inside a company team.
- Emphasize practical tooling, collaboration, and deployment readiness.

## Recommended structure
1. Header
2. Work-study-oriented summary
3. Two to three practical experiences
4. Applied projects
5. Education
6. Target stack

## Content priorities
- Prefer production-facing material: dashboards, pipelines, microservices, MLOps.
- Balance autonomy with structured academic progression.

## Final checks
- Fast onboarding signal is visible
- Team-relevant tooling is surfaced
- Tone feels dependable and execution-focused
