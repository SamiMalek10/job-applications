# Template — Internship (EN)

## Expected Format
- Short, focused internship cover letter.
- Professional tone with visible curiosity and readiness to contribute.

## Structure

### [HOOK]
- Open with a relevant technical result, project, or sharp observation about the internship scope.
- Avoid generic student openings.

### [STRONGEST_ALIGNMENT]
- Use the most transferable project or internship evidence.
- Show immediate contribution potential.

### [DIFFERENTIATOR]
- Emphasize learning speed, initiative, ownership, or a standout project.
- Reframe experience gaps through proof of execution.

### [CLOSING]
- Express motivation to learn from the team.
- Invite a conversation.
- End in a professional, concise way.

## Reminders
- Keep it compact.
- Focus on evidence, not adjectives.
