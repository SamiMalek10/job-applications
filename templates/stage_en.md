## Tone guidelines
- Tone: motivated, curious, learning-focused.
- Show evidence of fast learning and execution.

## Cover letter scaffold
1. **Opening**: why this specific company/mission is compelling.
2. **Body 1**: most relevant academic/technical project with measurable impact.
3. **Body 2**: skill-role fit + willingness to learn quickly in production context.
4. **Closing**: availability window and interview interest.

## CV priority order (internship)
1. Targeted profile summary
2. Education first
3. Relevant projects
4. Internships/short experiences
5. Technical skills

## EN conventions
- Direct and concise.
- Use hiring manager first name if known; otherwise formal neutral greeting.
- End with clear CTA for interview discussion.
