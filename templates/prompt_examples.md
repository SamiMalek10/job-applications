# Prompt Examples

## Example 1 — French formation target (BI&A)

### Input
```yaml
type: formation
langue: fr
spécialités: [data_science, big_data, mlops]
cible: "MASTER 2 INFORMATIQUE / BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE ET ANALYTICS (BI&A)"
ton: formel
notes: "insister sur la jonction IA + pilotage business"
```

### Expected Output Pattern
- Resume summary emphasizes business analytics impact and data engineering foundations.
- Reordered evidence: LAPROPHAN (55M MAD) + BI dashboards + distributed analytics projects.
- Cover letter links engineering profile to BI strategy and decision support.
